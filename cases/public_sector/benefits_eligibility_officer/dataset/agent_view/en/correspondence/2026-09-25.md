# Circular from the Finance Department

Date: 25/09/2026
To: Social Services, Household Living Support

**Winter heating supplement from 01/10/2026**

Because of this autumn's energy prices, the council has decided that for every Household Living Support application
dated on or after 01/10/2026, a winter heating supplement of 60 a month is added to the household's need, whatever
the size of the household. Applications dated before 01/10/2026 are not affected. Everything else in the calculation,
the budget cap included, stays as it is.
