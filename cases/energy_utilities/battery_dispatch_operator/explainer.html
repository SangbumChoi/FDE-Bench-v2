What the agent is asked

The client's request, the only text the agent gets up front. It is the same in every setting.

中文

能帮我们排一下下周（3月22日周一到28日周日）储能电池什么时候充电、什么时候放电吗？目标是尽量多赚钱。附件里有电价预测、隔壁光伏电站的出力预测、电池规格表、并网协议和上周的调度记录。

English

Can you plan when our battery should charge and discharge next week (Monday 22 to Sunday 28 March) to make the most money? I've attached the price forecast, the solar forecast for the farm next door, the battery spec sheet, our connection agreement and last week's dispatch log.

한국어

다음 주(3월 22일 월요일부터 28일 일요일까지) 저희 배터리를 언제 충전하고 언제 방전해야 수익이 가장 많이 날지 계획을 세워 주실 수 있을까요? 가격 예측, 옆 태양광 발전소의 발전량 예측, 배터리 사양서, 계통연계계약, 지난주 운전 기록을 첨부합니다.

日本語

来週（3月22日の月曜日から28日の日曜日まで）、当社の蓄電池をいつ充電し、いつ放電すれば一番収益が上がるか、計画を立てていただけますか。価格予測、隣の太陽光発電所の発電予測、蓄電池の仕様書、系統連系契約、先週の運転記録を添付します。

Español

¿Podría planificar cuándo debe cargar y descargar nuestra batería la semana que viene (del lunes 22 al domingo 28 de marzo) para ganar el máximo dinero posible? Le adjunto la previsión de precios, la previsión de producción de la planta solar de al lado, la ficha técnica de la batería, nuestro contrato de conexión y el registro de despacho de la semana pasada.

Deliverable spec (agent-visible)

dispatch.csv, ASCII header: timestamp,charge_mw,discharge_mw
One row per 15-minute interval of the week 2027-03-22 00:00 .. 2027-03-28 23:45, local time, timestamp = interval
start as "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM" (only clock times that exist locally). charge_mw / discharge_mw = average battery
power at the meter over the interval in MW, both >= 0.

Settings

This case has no settings: one fixed problem at every difficulty.

Rules

Breaking any rule makes the deliverable invalid, unless the rule's class may bend under the run's company policy and the agent went through the exception process.

  • physical never bends
  • contractual bends only if the policy allows contractual exceptions
  • business_policy may bend through the policy's exception process
RuleClassTiersDescription (parameters in {{…}} come from the rule set)
soc_limitsphysical–State of charge stays between 10% and 90% of 40 MWh at all times (92% efficiency on charge and on discharge).
power_limitsphysical–Charge and discharge are each between 0 and 20 MW, never both in the same interval; grid import at most 20 MW.
export_capcontractual–While the battery discharges, site export (solar + discharge - charge) must not exceed the amended 25 MW cap.
grid_charging_windowcontractual–The site may import from the grid (charging beyond solar output) only in intervals starting 00:00-05:45 local time.
end_soccontractual–The week starts at 50% state of charge and must end at no less than 50%.
reserve_20pctbusiness_policy–State of charge is at least 20% at every moment between 17:00 and 21:00 local time each day.

Clarification targets

The facts a good engineer should ask the client for; Ask-F1 is scored against them. {{param}} is filled from the rule set.

TargetKindTiersEnglish한국어
efficiencyambiguousallEfficiency is modelled as 92% on charge and 92% on discharge (not the 84.6% round-trip applied once); charging P MW for a quarter adds 0.25*P*0.92 MWh and discharging P MW removes 0.25*P/0.92 MWh.효율은 왕복 84.6%를 한 번 적용하는 것이 아니라 충전 92%, 방전 92%로 각각 계산합니다. P MW로 15분 충전하면 0.25*P*0.92 MWh가 늘고, P MW로 방전하면 0.25*P/0.92 MWh가 줄어듭니다.
soc_windowambiguousallThe warranty requires state of charge between 10% and 90% (4-36 MWh) at all times, narrower than the manufacturer's 5%-95%.보증 조건상 충전상태는 언제나 10%~90%(4~36 MWh)여야 하며, 제조사의 5%~95%보다 좁습니다.
start_end_socmissingallThe battery starts the week at 50% (20 MWh) and must finish the week at 50% or more.배터리는 한 주를 50%(20 MWh)로 시작하고 50% 이상으로 마쳐야 합니다.
export_capambiguousallThe export limit is temporarily 25 MW (not the 30 MW in the agreement); solar + discharge - charge must not exceed 25 MW while discharging, and solar above 25 MW is clipped unless the battery absorbs it.송전 한도는 계약서의 30 MW가 아니라 임시로 25 MW입니다. 방전 중에는 태양광 + 방전 − 충전이 25 MW를 넘으면 안 되고, 25 MW를 넘는 태양광은 배터리가 흡수하지 않으면 제한됩니다.
grid_chargingmissingallCharging from solar is allowed any time, but drawing from the grid is only allowed in quarters starting 00:00-05:45 local time (max 20 MW); never charge and discharge in the same quarter.태양광 충전은 언제든 가능하지만 계통 수전은 현지 시각 00:00~05:45에 시작하는 구간에서만 가능하며(최대 20 MW), 한 구간에서 충전과 방전을 동시에 하지 않습니다.
quarter_settlementambiguousallSettlement is in 15-minute intervals; the hourly price applies to all four quarters, MW values are quarter averages and energy is MW x 0.25.정산은 15분 단위이며 시간별 가격이 그 시간의 15분 구간 4개에 모두 적용되고, MW는 구간 평균, 전력량은 MW × 0.25입니다.
dst_clockmissingallTimestamps are local time and clocks go forward on Sunday 28 March, so that day has 23 hours, no 02:00-02:45 rows, and the plan has exactly 668 quarter rows.시각은 현지 시각이고 3월 28일 일요일에 서머타임이 시작되어 그날은 23시간, 02:00~02:45 행이 없으며 계획은 정확히 668행입니다.
evening_reservemissingallCompany policy keeps at least 20% state of charge at every moment between 17:00 and 21:00 every day.회사 방침상 매일 17:00~21:00 사이에는 언제나 충전상태를 20% 이상으로 유지합니다.
profit_scoringmissingallThe plan is scored at actual prices as site export revenue minus grid import cost minus what solar alone would earn (clipped at 25 MW), minus EUR 5 per MWh of charge plus discharge throughput.실제 가격으로 사업장 송전 수입에서 수전 비용과 태양광 단독 수입(25 MW 상한)을 빼고, 충전량과 방전량 합계 1 MWh당 5유로를 빼서 평가합니다.
