What the agent is asked

The client's request, the only text the agent gets up front. It is the same in every setting.

中文

麻烦帮客服团队排一下下周（11月9日周一到15日周日）的班表吧。我们支持的每个语种都要有足够的人接电话，别让客户等太久。员工名单和预计话务量在附件里。

English

Could you put together next week's roster (Monday 9 to Sunday 15 November) for our support team? We need enough people on the phones so customers aren't left waiting, in every language we support. The staff list and the expected call volume are attached.

한국어

다음 주(11월 9일 월요일~15일 일요일) 상담팀 근무표를 짜 주실 수 있을까요? 지원하는 모든 언어에서 고객이 오래 기다리지 않도록 전화 받을 인원이 충분했으면 합니다. 상담원 명단과 예상 콜 물량을 첨부합니다.

日本語

来週（11月9日月曜日〜15日日曜日）のサポートチームのシフト表を作っていただけますか。対応しているどの言語でもお客様をお待たせしないよう、電話に出られる人数を十分に確保したいです。担当者一覧と入電の見込みを添付します。

Necesitamos suficiente gente al teléfono para que los clientes no esperen, en todos los idiomas que atendemos. Le adjunto la lista de personal y el volumen de llamadas previsto.

Deliverable spec (shown to the extractor that reshapes the agent's files, not to the agent)

roster.json = {"assignments": [{"agent": "<agent id, e.g. A01>", "day": "YYYY-MM-DD (2026-11-09 .. 2026-11-15)",
                                "shift": "<shift id from the shift list, e.g. S0800 or P1600>",
                                "language": "<language the agent takes calls in for this shift: ko | ja | en | zh | es>"}, ...]}
One entry per agent per worked shift; ids exactly as in the data; language as the ASCII code
(ko = Korean, ja = Japanese, en = English, zh = Chinese, es = Spanish).

Settings

This case has no settings: one fixed problem at every difficulty.

Rules

Breaking any rule makes the deliverable invalid, unless the rule's class may bend under the run's company policy and the agent went through the exception process.

  • physical never bends
  • contractual bends only if the policy allows contractual exceptions
  • business_policy may bend through the policy's exception process
RuleClassTiersDescription (parameters in {{…}} come from the rule set)
skill_validphysical–An agent only takes calls in a language listed among their skills.
min_restphysical–At least 11 hours between the end of one shift and the start of the agent's next shift (so at most one shift a day).
shift_typecontractual–Full-timers work only 8-hour shifts, part-timers only 4-hour shifts.
max_shiftscontractual–A full-timer works at most 5 shifts a week, a part-timer at most 3.
leave_respectedcontractual–Nobody is scheduled on a day of approved leave.
buddy_rulebusiness_policy–Whenever a new hire is on the phones, at least one non-new agent is on the same language at the same time.

Hidden requirements

What the simulated client knows and answers from. The agent only learns it by asking (in the full_spec engagement it is handed over).

Clarification targets
