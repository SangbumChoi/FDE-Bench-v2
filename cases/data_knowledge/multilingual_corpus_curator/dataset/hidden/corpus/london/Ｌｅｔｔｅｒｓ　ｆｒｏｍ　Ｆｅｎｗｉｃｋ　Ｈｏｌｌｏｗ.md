# Ｌｅｔｔｅｒｓ　ｆｒｏｍ　Ｆｅｎｗｉｃｋ　Ｈｏｌｌｏｗ

by Marcus Ashdown and Eleanor Fairbairn

First published 1999 · This edition 2008

## About this book

For years a retired mapmaker has avoided the last station of an abandoned railway; then a letter arrives forty years late, and everything comes back.

In twelve short chapters that move between past and present, it asks about small kindnesses.
