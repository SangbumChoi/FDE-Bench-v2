# The Long Harbour — Stories from the Northern Coast and the Keepers Who Stayed Behind

by Eleanor Fairbairn

First published 1995 · This edition 2003

## About this book

When the migrating birds leave a month early, a newly arrived postman in a mountain village cut off by snow has to decide what is worth keeping.

In seventeen short chapters that move between past and present, it asks about how time changes a place.
