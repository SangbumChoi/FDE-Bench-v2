The Harbour at Marrow Bay — A Novel in Thirty-One Short Chapters
by Priya Ashdown
First published MCMLXXXVIII · This edition MCMXC

About this book

When the last train never comes back, a retired mapmaker in an old city of canals has to decide what is worth keeping.

In thirty-one short chapters that move between past and present, it asks about memory and forgiveness.
