# The Late Harbour

by Tobias Thorne and Clara Elsworth

First published 2002

## About this book

It begins in an island with a single bookshop: a letter arrives forty years late, and a newly arrived postman is the only one who remembers why.

A quiet novel about memory and forgiveness.
