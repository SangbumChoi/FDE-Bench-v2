# A Map of Aldermoor

by Tobias Ashdown

First published 2000 · This edition 2009

## About this book

When the migrating birds leave a month early, an apprentice clockmaker in a weather station on the high plateau has to decide what is worth keeping.

A quiet novel about home and elsewhere.
