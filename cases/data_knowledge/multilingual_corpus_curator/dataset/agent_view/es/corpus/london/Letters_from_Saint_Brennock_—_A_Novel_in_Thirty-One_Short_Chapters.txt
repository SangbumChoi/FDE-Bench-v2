Letters from Saint Brennock — A Novel in Thirty-One Short Chapters
by Priya Elsworth and Owen Hartigan
First published 2006 · This edition 2011

About this book

When the town clock stops at noon, a blind piano tuner in the last station of an abandoned railway has to decide what is worth keeping.

In thirty-one short chapters that move between past and present, it asks about what families leave unsaid.
