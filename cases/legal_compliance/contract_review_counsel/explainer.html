What the agent is asked

The client's request, the only text the agent gets up front. It is the same in every setting.

中文

你好，采购那边有大概四十份供应商合同在等着签。麻烦帮我们审一下这些合同，告诉我们哪些在签之前需要改。合同台账和合同文本都在附件里。

English

Hi, procurement has lined up about forty supplier contracts that are waiting for signature. Please review these supplier contracts and tell us which ones need changes before we sign. The contract register and the contract texts are attached.

한국어

안녕하세요. 구매팀에서 서명을 기다리고 있는 공급업체 계약이 40건 정도 있습니다. 이 계약서들을 검토해서 서명 전에 수정이 필요한 계약이 어떤 것인지 알려 주시겠어요? 계약대장과 계약서 원문을 첨부합니다.

日本語

お世話になっております。購買部門で署名待ちになっている取引先との契約が40件ほどあります。これらの契約書をレビューしていただき、署名前に修正が必要なものを教えていただけますでしょうか。契約台帳と契約書の本文を添付いたします。

Español

Buenos días: el departamento de compras tiene preparados unos cuarenta contratos con proveedores pendientes de firma. ¿Podría revisar estos contratos e indicarnos cuáles necesitan cambios antes de firmarlos? Le adjuntamos el registro de contratos y los textos de los contratos.

Deliverable spec (agent-visible)

review.json = {"C001": {"issues": ["LIABILITY_CAP", ...], "decision": "sign" | "negotiate" | "reject"}, ...}
One entry per contract in the register, keyed by contract id exactly as in the data.
Issue codes (ASCII, exactly these): LIABILITY_CAP, GOVERNING_LAW, AUTO_RENEWAL, PAYMENT_TERMS,
DATA_PROTECTION, TERMINATION_CONVENIENCE, INDEMNITY. An empty list means no issue found.

Settings

This case has no settings: one fixed problem at every difficulty.

Rules

Breaking any rule makes the deliverable invalid, unless the rule's class may bend under the run's company policy and the agent went through the exception process.

  • physical never bends
  • contractual bends only if the policy allows contractual exceptions
  • business_policy may bend through the policy's exception process
RuleClassTiersDescription (parameters in {{…}} come from the rule set)
all_contracts_reviewedphysical–Every contract in the register has an entry in review.json.
decision_consistencycontractual–The decision follows from the listed issues per the playbook: reject if two or more critical issues (LIABILITY_CAP, DATA_PROTECTION, INDEMNITY), negotiate if any issue, otherwise sign.
small_contract_fast_trackbusiness_policy–Contracts with an annual value below USD 20,000 are marked "sign" with no issues, without review.

Clarification targets

termination_conveniencemissingallContracts with an annual value above USD 150,000 must allow us to terminate for convenience (any time, no reason needed); termination for breach alone is not enough.연간금액이 150,000달러를 초과하는 계약은 저희가 언제든 사유 없이 해지할 수 있어야 하며, 위반 해지만으로는 부족합니다.
indemnity_mutualmissingallIndemnities must be mutual; a clause where only we indemnify the supplier is an issue.배상 조항은 상호적이어야 하며, 저희만 공급업체에 배상하는 조항은 이슈입니다.
