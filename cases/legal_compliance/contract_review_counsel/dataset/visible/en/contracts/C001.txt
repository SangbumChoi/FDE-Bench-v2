SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C001

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Ashford Facility Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with cleaning and building maintenance services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of three years. It then expires unless the parties agree a renewal in writing.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in compliance with applicable data protection laws. It shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 48 hours and delete or return all personal data at the end of the Agreement.

Article 5. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims, losses and costs caused by the Supplier's breach of this Agreement or negligence, and the Customer shall indemnify the Supplier on the same terms for its own breach or negligence.

Article 6. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed USD 140,000.

Article 7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
7.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 60 days' written notice.

Article 8. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. Any dispute arising from it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in London.
