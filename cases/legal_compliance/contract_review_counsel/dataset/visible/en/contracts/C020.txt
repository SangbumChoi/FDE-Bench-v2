SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C020

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Kestrel Facility Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with cleaning and building maintenance services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of two years. It then renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least forty-five days before the end of the current term.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 Undisputed invoices are payable net 45 from the date of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed USD 1,170,000.

Article 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute arising from it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in Zurich.
