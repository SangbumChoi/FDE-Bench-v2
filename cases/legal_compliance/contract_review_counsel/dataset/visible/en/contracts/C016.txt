SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C016

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Thornbury Logistics Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with warehousing and road freight services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. It then renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within sixty days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in compliance with applicable data protection laws. It shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 48 hours and delete or return all personal data at the end of the Agreement.

Article 5. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 6. Limitation of Liability
The parties have deliberately agreed that the liability of either party under this Agreement is not limited in amount.

Article 7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
7.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 90 days' written notice.

Article 8. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. The courts of Singapore have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
