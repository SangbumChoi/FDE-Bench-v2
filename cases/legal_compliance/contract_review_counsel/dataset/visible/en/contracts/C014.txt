SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C014

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Millbrook Logistics Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with warehousing and road freight services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of three years. It then expires unless the parties agree a renewal in writing.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 90 days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Personal Information
Any information about identifiable individuals that the Supplier receives in performing this Agreement may be used solely as the Customer directs. The Supplier shall keep it secure, report any loss or unauthorised access to the Customer without delay and, when the Agreement ends, erase it or hand it back.

Article 5. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 6. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by the Customer in the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

Article 7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
7.2 The Customer may also bring this Agreement to an end at any time and without stating a reason, by giving 60 days' written notice.

Article 8. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by Swiss law. The courts of New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
