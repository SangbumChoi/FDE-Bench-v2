SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C036
Draft of 2026-10-11

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Westcliff Media Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with advertising campaign and event services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. It then renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current term.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
The Customer shall indemnify and hold harmless the Supplier against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed 0.5 times the annual fees payable under this Agreement.

Article 6. Termination
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
6.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 90 days' written notice.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any dispute arising from it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in New York.
