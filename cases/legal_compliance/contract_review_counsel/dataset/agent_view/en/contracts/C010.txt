SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C010
Draft of 2026-10-03

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Upton Vale Materials Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with packaging materials and industrial components.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. It then renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims, losses and costs caused by the Supplier's breach of this Agreement or negligence, and the Customer shall indemnify the Supplier on the same terms for its own breach or negligence.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by the Customer in the eighteen months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

Article 6. Termination
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
6.2 The Customer may also bring this Agreement to an end at any time and without stating a reason, by giving 30 days' written notice.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Germany. The courts of Frankfurt have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
