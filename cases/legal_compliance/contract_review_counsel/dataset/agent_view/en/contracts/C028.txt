SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C028
Draft of 2026-09-23

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Penbury Digital Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with cloud hosting, software maintenance and related data services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of two years. At the end of each term the Agreement continues for a further year on the same conditions, unless a party objects in writing no later than 30 days before the term ends.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Data Protection
The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions and in compliance with applicable data protection laws. It shall implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures, notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 48 hours and delete or return all personal data at the end of the Agreement.

Article 5. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 6. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed one half of the annual fees payable under this Agreement.

Article 7. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.

Article 8. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Switzerland. Any dispute arising from it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in Zurich.
