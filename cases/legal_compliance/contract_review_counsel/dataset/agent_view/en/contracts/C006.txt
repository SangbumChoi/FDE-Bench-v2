SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C006
Draft of 2026-10-16

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Hartwell Consulting Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with consulting and translation services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. At the end of each term the Agreement continues for a further year on the same conditions, unless a party objects in writing no later than 45 days before the term ends.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 90 days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed US$440 thousand in aggregate.

Article 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore. The courts of Hong Kong have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
