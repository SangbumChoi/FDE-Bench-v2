SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C002
Draft of 2026-10-14

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Glenmore Logistics Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with warehousing and road freight services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. At the end of each term the Agreement continues for a further year on the same conditions, unless a party objects in writing no later than 90 days before the term ends.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 90 days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims, losses and costs caused by the Supplier's breach of this Agreement or negligence, and the Customer shall indemnify the Supplier on the same terms for its own breach or negligence.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed US$15 thousand in aggregate.

Article 6. Termination
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
6.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 30 days' written notice.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any dispute arising from it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in New York.
