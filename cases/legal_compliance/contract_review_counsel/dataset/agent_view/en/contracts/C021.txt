SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C021
Draft of 2026-09-23

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Larkfield Facility Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with cleaning and building maintenance services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. It then expires unless the parties agree a renewal in writing.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed twice the annual fees payable under this Agreement.

Article 6. Termination
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.
6.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time by giving 90 days' written notice.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by Swiss law. The courts of Zurich have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
