SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. C011
Draft of 2026-10-19

This Agreement is made between Brightvale Holdings (the "Customer") and Yarrow Media Ltd (the "Supplier").

Article 1. Scope and Term
1.1 The Supplier provides the Customer with advertising campaign and event services.
1.2 This Agreement starts on the date of signature and runs for an initial term of one year. It then expires unless the parties agree a renewal in writing.

Article 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 The fees are set out in the order forms agreed between the parties.
2.2 Undisputed invoices are payable net 15 from the date of receipt.

Article 3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all business information received from the other party and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. This obligation survives the end of the Agreement for three years.

Article 4. Indemnity
Each party shall indemnify the other party against all third-party claims, losses and costs arising from its own breach of this Agreement or its negligence.

Article 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for liability for death, personal injury or fraud, the total liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed 100% of the annual fees payable under this Agreement.

Article 6. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of being notified.

Article 7. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. The courts of New York have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
